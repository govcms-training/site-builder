# The features of GovCMS

GovCMS includes many features tailored for Australian government websites, including:

* A **robust theme layer** that allows for any kind of visual display to the users. See a list of websites using the GovCMS platform with a range of themes on the [GovCMS Gallery](https://www.govcms.gov.au/gallery)
* Customisable **content containers** to collect the right information in the right fields
* **Reusable content templates** to give content editors easy to use templates to add content around the website
* The ability to **manage media**, such as documents, photos and videos
* **Taxonomy** terms to categorise your content and provide useful filters for users
* **Text formats** to control the content that can be added by content authors
* **Custom lists** such as a compiled list of articles, media releases, publications or other types of content
* A flexible **URL pattern** system that allows for redirects on demand
* A simple **search** system to surface content to users
* A **performance and reliability** focused hosting platform
* **Content moderation** and workflow to allow content to go through review and approval processes before being published.
