# Overview

Site performance and caching are two important aspects of your website. However, most of the controls and settings are controlled at the platform level, by GovCMS.

There are a number of tests you can run, and best practices you can follow to optimise your website's performance, but settings like cache life time and server memory allocation are all controlled by GovCMS.

In the following two lessons we'll discuss [performance](performance-on-govcms.md) on GovCMS and how you can optimise your website for best results, as well as the various levels of [caching](caching-on-govcms.md) that take place on GovCMS.
