# Paragraphs

**Paragraphs**, sometimes called **components**, are repeatable content templates that content authors can pick and choose when creating their content.

You can think of them as mini content types, placed inside content types, just like another field.

And just like content types, they're built with **fields** and have the same **Manage form** and **Manage display** options.

In the Training environment we have CivicTheme installed, which comes with a number of prebuilt paragraphs such as:

* Accordion
* Promo
* Campaign
* Callout

To view the paragraphs, hover over **Structure** and select **Paragraph types**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (35).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You'll notice the first two items, **Accordion** and **Accordion panel**. This shows how paragraphs can also reference other paragraphs inside them.

**Accordion** is the wrapper or container, and **Accordion panel** is for the individual accordion items inside them.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (36).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>One accordion, with two accordion panels.</p></figcaption></figure>

### Considerations

Just like when building content types, it's important to **think about your requirements** for a paragraph. For example, a paragraph with just a single field is potentially not very useful.

You may also find existing paragraphs contain the functionality you need, or maybe the  functionality being requested is so niche and one-off that spending time making a reusable content template via paragraphs is not a good idea.
