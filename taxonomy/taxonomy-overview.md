# Taxonomy overview

Taxonomies are Drupal's way of categorising content. Other CMSes might call these **tags**, **topics** or **terms**.

In Drupal, we have taxonomy vocabularies that are **containers** for many taxonomy **terms**. These terms are then applied to our **content**.

An example would be the taxonomy **vocabulary** of “States and Territories" and then the taxonomy terms would be each of the **states/territories**.

There's no limit to the amount of **vocabularies** or **terms** that you have, however it's important to regularly audit them to make sure you haven’t accidentally created near-duplicates or introduced spelling mistakes.

### How can taxonomies be used?

Let's look at some examples of how taxonomies can be used on your website.

The most useful reason for categorising content with taxonomies is to make a **better** and more useful **search** feature.

Allowing users to filter search results based on content taxonomy terms can help them find related items without having to guess the best search term.
